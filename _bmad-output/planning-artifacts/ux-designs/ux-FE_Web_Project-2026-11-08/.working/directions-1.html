Authentication Structural & Personality Directions

Three distinct layout architectures for /login, /register, /forgot-password, and /reset-password. Every direction honors the YumeZone pitch-black (#000000) cinema foundation, translucent white/10 glass borders, glowing white primary CTAs, sage-teal (#91B8B1) highlights, and bold sans + italic serif typography across desktop down to 360px mobile viewports.

DIRECTION A Floating Cinema Portal Depicts: /login + /register (360px Error State)

Centered Atmospheric Glass Portal

Treats authentication like stepping through a darkened theater lobby: a full-bleed monochrome manga-panel backdrop fades into a deep #000000 vignette behind a centered 420px frosted-glass portal card. Balanced field density keeps focus on primary credential entry while preserving ambient anime/manga mood without competing with input legibility. On 360px mobile screens, the glass card fills the viewport width cleanly with zero horizontal overflow and crisp inline validation feedback.

DIRECTION C Zero-Chrome Spotlight Sheet Depicts: /login (Auth Error) + /forgot-password (Inbox Sent)

Zero-Chrome Spotlight Sheet

Strips away card borders and decorative artwork in favor of razor-sharp entry speed on a pure #000000 canvas lit only by an overhead rgba(145,184,177,0.10) radial spotlight. A segmented top pill switches routes instantaneously between Sign In and Create Account, while placing Sign in with Google first above a tight email rhythm minimizes time-to-stream. Because the sheet is natively 376px max-width with zero outer card chrome, desktop and 360px mobile share an identical, ultra-fast vertical flow.
